Process and implementation assumptions for your review

  • Email identity: show the service provider as issuer and TRISE as the secure platform. Route quotation discussion through the authenticated portal conversation.
  • Secure link: open the existing customer sign-in; after authentication, return to this exact quotation revision and check customer-relationship access. Sign-in does not record a decision.
  • Decision: acceptance requires an explicit second confirmation for the immutable revision. “Request changes” creates a separate revision request; it does not accept or alter the issued quote.
  • Template: render the provider logo, business name, configured address, and enabled contact details from the issued letterhead snapshot. Show customer-visible scope and terms; omit excluded lines and internal configuration.
  • Conversation: show the quotation thread only after sign-in; keep it open while awaiting acceptance, then lock new messages after acceptance.
  • Authorization: existing customer authentication is Google-based. The mock uses that sign-in, not a new password or email OTP method.
