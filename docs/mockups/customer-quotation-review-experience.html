Design and lifecycle rules shown in this preview

  • Customer contact, verified portal identity, and relationship authorization are distinct states.
  • Provider approval and customer acceptance are separate decisions, each tied to the exact quotation revision.
  • A mismatched invited account sees no customer or quotation details; the flow offers a safe account switch.
  • Customer acceptance records actor, role, timestamp and revision. It does not create a contract, start coverage, or prove payment.
  • The provider creates and activates the paid AMC contract; the customer can then see the resulting coverage in their own portal.
  • All sample values and interaction state are local to this preview. Product permissions and status must come from the API.
